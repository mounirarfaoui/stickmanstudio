# FLOOR 0 — a stickman elevator horror series where the comments decide

The story runs 20+ parts (the user asked on 2026-11-01 for it to go as long as possible; see the arc below). Every part is under 60 s and ends on a
two-option choice. The voice-over asks "Comment A or B"; the end card says "Follow so you don't miss the next floor".
The next part is made from the majority vote, so parts are produced one at a time.

## Rules of the world
- **Theo** (black stickman, narrator, voice *Fenrir*) wakes in an elevator with buttons 1–100 and no 0.
- **The Operator** (voice *Enceladus*, intercom "tv" filter) is never seen: "Find Floor Zero, and you may leave."
- The doors stay open **30 seconds** on every floor; the DOORS CLOSE timer is on screen.
- Three bulbs on the ceiling = three lives. A bad choice blows one out; all three out = dark ending. Lights can be earned back (Part 3), so the story can run long.
- Every floor is a different world. From Part 2 on, open with a 2-second flash of what the losing option would have done.
- Planted mysteries: someone before Theo scratched tally marks and "DON'T TRUST HER" next to the notice;
  the school drawings show Theo in this elevator, and a tall scribbled figure beside it.

## The long arc (20+ parts, adjusted to every vote)

Every act can stretch with extra floors, as long as viewers keep voting.

- **Act 1, Who is Theo? (Parts 1–6):** the Other Theo, the "last" Theo he hurt, and the first time one of them dies.
- **Act 2, The copies (Parts 7–12):** a floor full of Theos. Each one woke in the same fall. The tallies are not days but tries. The girl returns, and she has been helping someone.
- **Act 3, The Operator (Parts 13–18):** Floor 99, the control room, and the voice's real face. A second elevator with its own Theo and its own voters.
- **Act 4, Floor 0 (Parts 19–24+):** how to reach a floor with no button, what the key opens, who the viewers really are to Theo, and the final vote.
