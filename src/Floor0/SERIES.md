# FLOOR 0 — a stickman elevator horror series where the comments decide

Season 1 is planned at about 10 parts (8–12 depending on the votes). Every part is under 60 s and ends on a
two-option choice. The voice-over asks "Comment A or B"; the end card says "Follow so you don't miss the next floor".
The next part is made from the majority vote, so parts are produced one at a time.

## Rules of the world
- **Theo** (black stickman, narrator, voice *Fenrir*) wakes in an elevator with buttons 1–100 and no 0.
- **The Operator** (voice *Enceladus*, intercom "tv" filter) is never seen: "Find Floor Zero, and you may leave."
- The doors stay open **30 seconds** on every floor; the DOORS CLOSE timer is on screen.
- Three bulbs on the ceiling = three lives. A bad majority choice blows one out; all three out = dark ending.
- Every floor is a different world. From Part 2 on, open with a 2-second flash of what the losing option would have done.
- Planted mysteries: someone before Theo scratched tally marks and "DON'T TRUST HER" next to the notice;
  the school drawings show Theo in this elevator, and a tall scribbled figure beside it.
