# LAST LIGHT — a stickman zombie apocalypse in 10 parts

Ten vertical shorts, ~30 s each, narrated first-person by **Kai** (black stickman, ordinary guy, quiet humour,
gets braver every episode). Every part opens on a hook line and ends on a cliffhanger plus "PART N+1".

Visual language: dark blue night interiors, blood-orange sky outside, zombies as grey-green stickmen with
glowing green eyes. The "light" motif — a lamp, a lighter, headlights, the lighthouse — marks hope in every part.

| # | Title | What happens | Cliffhanger |
|---|-------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Day One** | Kai is making coffee when an emergency broadcast tells everyone to stay inside. Across the street his neighbour Mr. Dawson walks *wrong*, looks up at Kai's window and charges. Kai barricades the door through the pounding — then silence. | Behind him, the window is covered in hands. It was never just one. |
| 2 | **The Window** | The glass gives. Kai escapes through the bathroom window onto the fire escape and drops into an alley full of abandoned cars. | A car door opens on its own — someone is hiding inside. |
| 3 | **Mia** | Inside the car is Mia, a teenager with a bat and a dead phone, waiting for a mother who never came. They don't trust each other; they need each other. | A dog barks at the end of the alley — and every zombie turns toward it. |
| 4 | **The Horde** | The barking pulls the horde into the street. Kai, Mia and the dog (Biscuit) climb to a rooftop and watch a river of the dead flow past for hours. | Mia's walkie-talkie crackles: a voice says "...lighthouse..." |
| 5 | **The Signal** | They scavenge batteries in a dark pharmacy. Fully powered, the radio repeats a message: *safe zone, the lighthouse at Gull Point, 50 miles north. Lights on at night.* | The pharmacy door bell rings. They are not alone. |
| 6 | **Strangers** | Two armed survivors offer a ride north in their truck. Kai says yes; Mia says no. | At the first stop the strangers drive off with their bags — and Biscuit. |
| 7 | **The Bridge** | On foot, they reach the only bridge north. It's jammed with cars and the dead. They cross on the car roofs, jumping gap to gap. | Kai slips. A hand closes around his ankle. |
| 8 | **The Scratch** | Mia pulls him free, but there's a scratch on his leg. The night is long: Kai writes her a note in case he turns by morning. | Dawn. He opens his eyes. They're still his. Then they hear barking — Biscuit, alone, running toward them. |
| 9 | **Gull Point** | They reach the lighthouse. No lights. The gate is open, the beds are empty, the radio message is on a loop. There never was a safe zone. | The horde from the bridge followed them. It's at the gate. |
| 10 | **Last Light** | Kai and Mia climb the tower, fix the generator and switch the lamp on. It doesn't save the world — but across the dark coast, other lights blink back. They aren't the last. | Final line: "The world ended on a Tuesday. We decided it didn't get to end us." |

## Part 1 — Day One (beat sheet, story frames @ 30 fps)

| Story frames | Beat | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0–90 | Kai at the kitchen counter pouring coffee. Title card. | "The day the world ended... I was making coffee." |
| 90–210 | The TV cuts to an emergency broadcast: red banner, static, alert tone. | (Anchor, through the TV) "Emergency alert. Stay indoors. Lock your doors. Do not let anyone in." |
| 210–390 | Kai drifts to the window. Across the street Mr. Dawson twitches and lurches. | "Then I saw Mr. Dawson across the street." / "He was walking... wrong." |
| 390–500 | Dawson's head snaps up, eyes glow. He charges. Kai drops the mug — it shatters. | "He looked up. Right at me." |
| 500–660 | Kai drags the dresser across the door. Pounding — the door jumps, the camera shakes. | "I barricaded the door." / "He hit it. Again. And again." |
| 660–760 | The pounding stops. Kai slides down the dresser, breathing. The lamp flickers. | "Then... silence." |
| 760–850 | Whip-pan to the window: dozens of glowing eyes, hands on the glass, a crack spreading. | "That's when I realised... it was never just him." |
| 850–900 | Black. LAST LIGHT — PART 1/10. "Part 2 soon." | — |
